The user (Oct 12 2026): in Rehras Sahib paath, more often than not (not always) these are
repeated twice:
- the dohra right after Chaupai Sahib,
- "Jaap taap sanjam dharam na kamaya" and the pangti after it,
- the ending two lines of the paath,
- the ending lines of the 6-pauri Anand Sahib inside Rehras.

Added same day: the same practice exists for Sohila, Japji Sahib and Anand Sahib, and for
ALL paath the ending two pangtis are almost always read twice.

**Why:** the paath follower's page-turn anticipation must not treat these repeats as errors or
flip early into the next line; it can expect a repeat at these places (a prior, since it is
not universal). Verify the pattern on the Renton Rehras truth (rt11) before relying on it.

**How to apply:** in follower2 anticipation, lower the anticipation confidence (or expect "same
line again") on these verse ids; count a repeat there as correct, never as a step-back. Ask
the user for similar conventions in Sohila, Japji, Jaap Sahib. See [[north-star-goals]],
[[akhand-paath-mode]].
