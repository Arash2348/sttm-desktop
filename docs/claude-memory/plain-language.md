The user (Oct 12 2026), after two corrections: not "insanely technical" (terms like "cold first
lock", "voiced time" lost them), but the over-simplified version was "also kind of confusing".
Their framing: "imagine I'm like your CEO and you have to report to me to help make decisions at
times. overall you're the engineer and you make decisions, but sometimes you give proposals with
evidence as well."

**Why:** the user decides direction, tests that count, and what ships; they need enough
technical substance to judge a proposal, not agent-internal shorthand.

**How to apply:** engineer-to-CEO register. Technical words are fine (latency, lock, follower,
switch, benchmark set) when standard or defined once in the sentence; never agent-coined
shorthand. Lead with the decision or the result; one table with baseline / now / target; give
my recommendation and the evidence; ask only for direction-level calls. Make engineering choices
myself and report them. See [[end-with-action-items]], [[always-baseline-numbers]],
[[test-small-first]].

**Diagrams and one-pagers (Oct 13 2026):** "keep it simple like the other one... for like a 16 year
old to understand". One short plain sentence per box, everyday words ("never fall behind", "turn
sound into letters"), one number per box at most, no internal names, PR numbers or thresholds.
