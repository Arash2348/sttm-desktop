STTM Voice Autopilot — how it works (Option A)

Current experimental build, Voice Autopilot 9.0.5. Blue = hear · green = find · amber = follow · red = switch · teal = what people see.

flowchart TD
  classDef hear fill:#e8f1fb,stroke:#2b6cb0,color:#1d2127
  classDef find fill:#e9f6ee,stroke:#2f855a,color:#1d2127
  classDef follow fill:#fdf3e1,stroke:#b7791f,color:#1d2127
  classDef sw fill:#fdeaea,stroke:#c53030,color:#1d2127
  classDef out fill:#e6f5f5,stroke:#2c7a7b,color:#1d2127
  classDef dec fill:#ffffff,stroke:#4a5568,color:#1d2127

  MIC([Mic / mixer audio]):::hear --> AW[Audio thread
2048-sample chunks]:::hear
  AW --> Q[Backlog queue
merge if more than 1 s waiting
nothing dropped]:::hear
  Q --> REC[Recognizer every 0.5 s
last 10 s while searching
last 4 s while following]:::hear
  REC --> MODEL[Speech model - CTC, int8
greedy decode]:::hear
  MODEL --> HYP[/Heard text + confidence/]:::hear
  HYP --> PH{Which phase?}:::dec

  PH -- searching --> SB[Same heard text goes to
two searches side by side]:::find
  SB --> S1[Full-text search
matches whole words, 3-letter pieces
top 3 Shabads]:::find
  SB --> S2[First-letter search
matches first letters of words
8,6,5,4-letter pieces, voting]:::find
  S1 --> R1{Mool Mantar?
4 hits on Shabad 1}:::dec
  R1 -- no --> R2{Jaap / Aarti pool?
5 of last 8 searches}:::dec
  R2 -- no --> R3{Sound check?
leaders scored vs audio
3 agreeing votes}:::dec
  R3 -- no --> R4{Text tally?
6+ hits, 4+ wins
2+ pangtis, margin 0.08}:::dec
  S2 --> R5{First-letter lead?
65% share, 8+ votes
2 decodes + text agrees}:::dec
  R1 -- yes --> LOCK
  R2 -- yes --> LOCK
  R3 -- yes --> LOCK
  R4 -- yes --> LOCK
  R5 -- yes --> LOCK
  R4 -- no --> WAIT([keep listening]):::find
  R5 -- no --> WAIT

  LOCK[[LOCK a Shabad]]:::find --> BANI{Is it part of a Bani?
pangti only in one Bani,
2 Shabads in Bani order,
or 3 pangtis read in order}:::dec
  BANI -- yes --> OB[Open the Bani
at that pangti]:::find
  BANI -- no --> OS[Open the Shabad
at that pangti]:::find
  OB --> SCREEN
  OS --> SCREEN

  PH -- following --> F{Shabad or Bani?}:::dec
  F -- Shabad --> LF[Line follower
fuzzy match each pangti
next pangti easy, jumps need proof]:::follow
  F -- Bani --> PF[Position follower
align heard letters along the Bani]:::follow
  LF --> GATE{Move the screen?
confidence 0.4+, no switch building,
sevadaar not just clicked}:::dec
  PF --> GATE
  GATE -- yes --> SCREEN[(Screen: Shabad / pangti
for the sangat)]:::out
  GATE -- no --> HOLD([hold current pangti]):::follow

  PH -- following --> J[Switch judge
3 slots: first-letter, full-text,
Shabad just left]:::sw
  J --> W{Candidate beats current?
0.65 and +0.15 gives 1 win
0.80 and +0.30 gives 2 wins
else minus 1}:::dec
  W -- 2+ wins --> WAHE[ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ slide while unsure]:::out
  W -- 3 wins, or 2 to return --> LOCK
  W -- not yet --> HOLD
  TAP([Sevadaar taps a candidate]):::out --> LOCK
