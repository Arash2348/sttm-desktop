STTM Voice Autopilot — how it works (Option A)

Current experimental build, Voice Autopilot 9.0.5. Each box: what it means, in plain words, then the real numbers. Blue = hear · green = find · amber = follow · red = switch · teal = what people see.

  classDef hear fill:#e8f1fb,stroke:#2b6cb0,color:#1d2127
  classDef find fill:#e9f6ee,stroke:#2f855a,color:#1d2127
  classDef follow fill:#fdf3e1,stroke:#b7791f,color:#1d2127
  classDef sw fill:#fdeaea,stroke:#c53030,color:#1d2127
  classDef out fill:#e6f5f5,stroke:#2c7a7b,color:#1d2127
  classDef dec fill:#ffffff,stroke:#4a5568,color:#1d2127

  MIC(["Sound from the mic or mixer"]):::hear --> AW["Collect the sound
on its own thread so the app never stutters
2048-sample chunks"]:::hear
  AW --> Q["Never fall behind
if the PC is slow, waiting sound is joined up, not thrown away
merge when more than 1 s is waiting"]:::hear
  Q --> REC["Listen to the last few seconds
longer while looking for the Shabad, shorter while following it
10 s window searching · 4 s following · every 0.5 s"]:::hear
  REC --> MODEL["Turn sound into Gurmukhi letters
Karanbir Singh's speech model
CTC model, int8, greedy decode"]:::hear
  MODEL --> HYP[/"What was just sung or read
rough text, never shown on screen"/]:::hear
  HYP --> PH{"Do we know the Shabad yet?"}:::dec

  PH -- "no: searching" --> SB["Look it up two ways at once
each catches mistakes the other misses"]:::find
  SB --> S1["Full-text search
compare the heard words with every pangti
3-letter pieces · top 3 Shabads"]:::find
  SB --> S2["First-letter search
like STTM's own search box: first letter of each word
8/6/5/4-letter pieces · votes add up over time"]:::find
  S1 --> R1{"Is it Mool Mantar?
Shabad 1 on top 4 times"}:::dec
  R1 -- no --> R2{"Is it Jaap Sahib or Aarti?
many short Shabads of one Bani keep coming up
5 of the last 8 searches"}:::dec
  R2 -- no --> R3{"Does the audio really sound like it?
check the top 3 against the sound itself
3 agreeing checks over 2+ s"}:::dec
  R3 -- no --> R4{"Has the same Shabad kept winning?
steady winner across many tries, more than one pangti
6+ hits · 4+ wins · 2+ pangtis · clear lead"}:::dec
  S2 --> R5{"Do first letters clearly point to one Shabad?
and full-text agrees, as a double check
65% of votes · 8+ votes · 2 tries in a row"}:::dec
  R1 -- yes --> LOCK
  R2 -- yes --> LOCK
  R3 -- yes --> LOCK
  R4 -- yes --> LOCK
  R5 -- yes --> LOCK
  R4 -- no --> WAIT(["Keep listening
screen unchanged"]):::find
  R5 -- no --> WAIT

  LOCK[["LOCK: this is the Shabad"]]:::find --> BANI{"Is it part of a Bani being read?
pangti only in one Bani, two Shabads in Bani order,
or 3 pangtis read in order"}:::dec
  BANI -- yes --> OB["Open the whole Bani
e.g. Rehras, Japji, Aarti, at that pangti"]:::find
  BANI -- no --> OS["Open the Shabad
at the pangti being sung"]:::find
  OB --> SCREEN
  OS --> SCREEN

  PH -- "yes: following" --> F{"Shabad or Bani?"}:::dec
  F -- Shabad --> LF["Find the pangti being sung
moving to the next pangti is easy; jumping back or far needs more proof
4 s window · next 1 try · back 2 · far 3"]:::follow
  F -- Bani --> PF["Track our place in the Bani
like a finger moving along the Gutka as it is read
4 pangtis back / 6 ahead"]:::follow
  LF --> GATE{"Safe to move the screen?
sure enough, no switch underway,
sevadaar did not just click
confidence 0.4+ · 12 s respect after a click"}:::dec
  PF --> GATE
  GATE -- yes --> SCREEN[("Sangat screen
Shabad and pangti")]:::out
  GATE -- no --> HOLD(["Hold the current pangti"]):::follow

  PH -- "yes: following" --> J["Watch for a new Shabad
3 candidates: from first letters, from full text,
and the Shabad we just left (parmaan detour)"]:::sw
  J --> W{"Does a new Shabad fit the singing better?
points for clearly better, minus a point otherwise
+1 if 0.65 and ahead by 0.15 · +2 if 0.80 and ahead by 0.30"}:::dec
  W -- "2 points: unsure" --> WAHE["Show ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ slide
better than a wrong Shabad"]:::out
  W -- "3 points (2 to go back)" --> LOCK
  W -- "not yet" --> HOLD
  TAP(["Sevadaar taps a suggestion"]):::out --> LOCK
