STTM Voice Autopilot — how the harness works

Current experimental build (Voice Autopilot 9.0.5, KhalisFoundation/sttm-desktop experimental-release). Every condition below is the real threshold in the code (www/main/addons/voice-follow/). The draft 10.0.x work (Pankti Cumulation, fragment confirm) is not in this build.

1 · Audio in → text (every 0.5 s)

Mic / mixer1 channel, echo cancel + noise suppression off; falls back to default mic
→
AudioWorklet2048-sample chunks on the audio thread
→
Backlog queueone serial loop; if > 1.0 s waits, chunks are merged into one push (0.5 s on slowest tier) — nothing dropped
→
Resample → 16 kHzlinear
→
Recognizer window10 s while searching, 4 s while following; hop 0.5 s; reset on every lock/switch
→
Speech modelKaranbir Singh's CTC model, int8 ONNX (~184 MB), onnxruntime; greedy decode → Gurmukhi text + confidence (mean top-token prob)
→
Hypothesis"what was just sung/read" — never shown as Gurbani; used only to search and score
Speed tiers (only while following a read Bani): fast / sync / lean / min chosen by a 2-second self-test at load; step down if backlog > 0.5 s for 3 s, step up after 30 s of headroom. Kirtan always runs fast.

Never goes back to searching by itself: if the follower loses the line, the screen just holds. Stop never moves the screen. A manual tap on a candidate locks it and vetoes the Shabad tapped away from for 120 decodes. If the sevadaar clicks a line, the follower jumps there and for 12 s only moves to the next pangti (or on strong evidence). Background "hidden run" does the same silently (Shadower) and is adopted on Start.

3 · Finding the first Shabad — checked on every decode, in this order

Inputs: full-text search (character 3-grams over every pangti in the database, IDF-weighted, best pangti per Shabad, top 3 distinct Shabads) and first-letter search (Gurmukhi first letters of the words heard).
  1. Mool Mantar home — top hit is Shabad 1 verse 1 (score ≥ 0.6, exact tie) on 4 distinct hypotheses → lock Mool Mantar.
  2. Pooled Bani (Jaap Sahib, Aarti) — full-text top (≥ 0.2) belongs to that Bani in 5 of the last 8 searches (4 for Aarti) → lock.
  3. Sound confirmation — text top ≥ 0.3 and margin ≥ 0.08 → score the 3 leaders' pangtis against the last 10 s of audio (CTC). 3 agreeing votes (lead ≥ 0.05) over ≥ 4 decodes → lock.
  4. Context tally (last 24 decodes) — same Shabad on top: count ≥ 6, wins ≥ 4, margin ≥ 0.08, ≥ 2 different pangtis (or one very strong hit ≥ 0.8), summed score beats runner-up by ≥ 1 → lock.
  5. First-letter voting (≥ 4 letters) — up to 16 queries: start-anchored first 8 letters (weight 12), sliding 8/6/5/4-letter pieces (weight = length), one-letter-dropped variants; 8 results each; votes decay ×0.8 per decode, capped at 60.
  6. Pooled first-letter (Bani members) — Bani's Shabads sum ≥ 8 votes with ≥ 65% share and full text agrees → lock best member after 3 stable decodes.
  7. First-letter lock — ≥ 5 letters, leader ≥ 65% of (leader+runner-up), ≥ 8 votes, same leader 2 decodes, and full-text search agrees (margin ≥ 0.08) → lock.
Same Gurbani in two places (copies) counts as one Shabad when picking leaders.

3b · Opening a Bani instead of a single Shabad

  1. Paath-only pangti: the locked pangti exists in exactly one of Japji, Jaap, Anand, Rehras, Sohila, Chaupai, Aarti… (not Gur Mantar / Sohila / Ardas, not pangtis 1–4) → open that Bani at that pangti.
  2. Two Shabads in order: the new Shabad comes within 3 positions after the previous one in the same Bani → open the Bani.
  3. Reading rule: 3 different pangtis read in order inside a Shabad of Rehras / Anand / Japji / Aarti → open that Bani. Stepping back (sung, not read) on an Aarti piece → open Aarti there.
Rehras and Aarti open at the extra-long length; the length is restored afterwards.

4 · Following the pangti

Shabad → line follower4 s window, every 0.25 s. Each pangti scored by fuzzy match to what was heard. Ignore the decode if best < 0.55 or top-2 gap < 0.10. Path over pangtis (Viterbi): next pangti cheap (0.03), step back 0.14, jump 0.28, rahao +0.18, revisit +0.10. First line needs 4 agreeing decodes; next pangti 1 decode, back 2, far jump 3 (and ≥ 8 letters). 14 bad decodes in a row → let go (screen holds).
Bani → position followerWhole Bani as one letter stream (spoken form). Each window aligned locally (Smith-Waterman); search 4 pangtis back / 6 ahead. Lock 0.6 over 3 windows; new pangti needs gain 3; skip gain 6; step back 4 windows; far jump margin 0.15 over 4 windows; catch-up when 4 windows match further ahead. Asa di Vaar has looser back/skip rules.
Writes to the screen only if confidence ≥ 0.4, no switch is building (no slot has a win), the row is shown in this Bani length, and the sevadaar hasn't just clicked (12 s respect window).

6 · What people see

7 · Safety

Stale work cancelled by session counters and abort signals · bad audio chunk skipped · backlog bounded (merge + speed tiers) · search runs in a separate process (5 s timeout, back-off, "search not available" message) · limited model threads · hidden run pauses when the PC is busy (CPU > 90%) · failed switch keeps the old Shabad.
