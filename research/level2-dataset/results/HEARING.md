# Hearing score: is hard audio lost by the speech model or by the app's logic?

Oct 14 2026. A quick diagnostic for Voice Autopilot on the two hard tiers (GAB-Hard: Fremont hall, laptop mic;
GAB-Akhand Kirtan: many singers, sangat singing along) with main GAB (mixer feed) as the control. Six model forms of
three speech models hear the same 4 s windows. No app logic is involved in the hearing numbers.

## Verdict

| tier | where the misses come from | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Main GAB (control) | Neither: works | The app shows the right Shabad 94.5% of the time. Only 0.8% of windows had the right Shabad winning the model's own 30 s vote while the app showed something else |
| GAB-Hard | **The model.** No model hears it | Per-window hit@5 is 4-7% for every model and the 30 s vote is right 7-12%, yet the app shows the right Shabad 52% of the time. Of the time the app is wrong or blank, the model's vote had the right Shabad only 3% of the time. On kh11 and kh13 (app 0%) all six model forms vote right 0% of the time |
| GAB-Akhand Kirtan | **Mostly the model**, with one logic loss | The app is right 74.6% of the time, the vote 33.7%. Of the time the app is wrong or blank, the vote had it 12% of the time (3.1% of all windows). 56 of those 62 windows are one clip, ytak_0typ: the app locked a wrong Shabad (2870) at 9.7 s, put up the Waheguru slide at 48.6 s, and showed 3375 (sung from 36 s, the model's vote already right) only at 95.2 s. That is a find/switch logic loss after a wrong first lock. Elsewhere evidence is rarely lost: on ytak_tD1 (app 9.8%) every model votes right about 10% of the time |

**The logic is not throwing evidence away.** On every tier the app shows the right Shabad far more often than any
logic-free reading of the model's output supports: 36-47% of all windows are "shown right, without a right vote".
The follower holds a Shabad through stretches the model cannot hear. Where the app fails on hard audio, the model
gave it almost nothing to work with.

**No model is clearly better at hearing.**
- **Sukhpal's (CTC)** hears best on Akhand Kirtan: hit@5 16.0 vs 12.0, usable text 58.6% vs 47.3%, and l3clip01 hit@5
  18.6 vs 6.3. It is also slightly better on main (34.8 vs 32.7). But its 30 s vote is wrong more often on Akhand
  (31.9% vs 19.6%).
- **Surinder's (CTC)** is best on Hard (hit@5 6.6 vs 4.5, vote 11.8 vs 7.4) but votes wrong 24% of the time
  (Karanbir 11%). It is worse on main (27.9 vs 32.7).
- **Karanbir fp32 = int8** (within noise): the shipped int8 export costs nothing.
- **RNNT decoding**, which Surinder's card recommends, is worse than CTC on 4 s windows for both NeMo models: it emits
  little text (usable 20-55%). It is shown for completeness.

None of the models moves Hard from "not heard" to "heard". A model swap would not fix GAB-Hard. Akhand Kirtan would
gain at most a few points of evidence (Sukhpal's), at the cost of more wrong evidence.

## Results (tables generated by `hearing-score.js`; window-weighted; 7 clips x 5 min per tier)

Retrieval check: on 581 of 581 `text-retrieval` events in the app traces (clip01, kh12, l3clip26), `TextIndex` over the
corpus snapshot used here returns exactly the app's candidates, in the same order. So hit@k is the app's own search.

Definitions (per 4 s window with Shabad truth; hop 1 s):
- **hit@1 / hit@5**: the true Shabad is the top candidate, or among the top 5 distinct Shabads, of the app's own
  canonical retrieval (engine `TextIndex`, the app's corpus snapshot) on that window's transcript. Copies of the same
  Gurbani count (the app's `sameGurbani`). Fewer than 8 letters counts as a miss, the search phase's own minimum.
- **usable**: the transcript has 8 or more letters.
- **30 s vote**: over the windows ending in the last 30 s, the Shabad most often top-1 (at least 3 votes and a strict
  leader). A logic-free use of the model's hearing over time. "Wrong" means a different Shabad wins.
- **pangti sim**: the app's `fuzz.partialRatio` (0-100) of the transcript against the true pangti(s) sung in the window.
  "All windows" counts unusable windows as 0; "usable" scores only windows that heard text. "Chance" is the same
  transcript against an unrelated pangti, as a baseline.

so its pangti row rests on 93 windows.

## What could still be unfair

- **Training overlap**:
  - Surinder's 300 h kirtan set (297 YouTube videos, `video_id` read from all 129 parquet shards) contains none of
    the 17 GAB YouTube recordings checked (every video in this sample plus the other L2 ones).
  - Sukhpal's `speech-data-v1` includes Darbar Sahib sessions (2019-2026, named by ragi and date). Main clips clip12
    and clip17 are Darbar Sahib broadcasts and can't be matched without upload dates, so overlap there is possible.
    His set also has Sri Raag, Gauri and Aasa raag shabad audio.
  - Karanbir's training data is unknown.
  - The Fremont (Hard) recordings are private STTM sessions, so they are unlikely to be in anyone's data.
- **Locks before the truth starts (Hard)**: kh12 (app 100%) and kh15 (96%) carry most of Hard's 52%.
  - kh12: the app locked at 20.5 s, inside the unlabelled lead-in (the sevadaar's screen starts at 43.7 s, and is not
    scored here). In those 40 lead-in windows Karanbir's text had the true Shabad in the top 5 once (Surinder 4,
    Sukhpal 2). The lock came from very thin evidence, partly from the first-letter route (not simulated here), and was
    then held.
  - "The app beats the model's own evidence" on Hard is therefore partly "locked early on a little evidence and held".
    That sharpens the verdict rather than changing it: Hard is short of hearing.
- **Window length**: 4 s windows are short for RNNT (it emits little), and the app itself uses 10 s. The app's own 10 s
  text scores about the same as 4 s on this sample.
- **Retrieval**: only the app's canonical text retrieval is used. The first-letter BaniDB route and the acoustic
  check are not simulated (same caveat as search-sim.js). Both help the app, which is part of why Y > X.
- **Truth quality**: Hard line truth is the sevadaar's screen (human, but it lags the singing). Akhand line truth is the
  live stream's slides (operator-run, parked on long holds). Main has line truth on one aligner-made clip.
  Shabad-level truth is human or slides on every clip.
- **Sample size**: 7 clips x 5 min per tier, each cut from t=0 (cold start, as GAB runs).
  - Akhand: l3clip01/26 are train split, ytak_tD1/zi5p/kB3 dev, ytak_0typ/Q5QF test. This is a diagnostic only;
    nothing was tuned (Level-3 policy).
  - Hard: practice split only (kh01-kh10 held out).

## Rerun / add a model (one command)

```
cd ~/personal/sttm-replay/research/level2-dataset/tools
./hearing.sh <model>          # transcribe the sample (skips clips already done), then rescore everything
./hearing.sh score            # rescore only -> results/hearing/hearing-scores.{md,json}
./hearing.sh app              # app-side traces for the akhand tier (production model, private store)
```

`<model>` is one of `karanbir-int8 | karanbir-fp32 | surinder-ctc | surinder-rnnt | sukhpal-ctc | sukhpal-rnnt`, or:
- `nemo:/path/model.nemo[:ctc|rnnt]` for any AI4Bharat IndicConformer-style hybrid checkpoint (multisoftmax; the pa
  slice is found from the checkpoint's own config and tokenizer). Jaspal's model plugs in here if it is a NeMo
  fine-tune of the same base.
- `onnx:/path/model.onnx` with `HEARING_SPM=/path/tokenizer.model` for a Karanbir-style ONNX (inputs `audio`,
  `audio_len`; outputs `log_probs`, `out_len`; blank 256).

Set `HEARING_NAME=<folder>` to name a generic model's results. Any other format needs one small class in
`hearing-infer.py` with `batch(list of 16 kHz float32 4 s windows) -> list of Gurmukhi strings`, and an entry in
`load()`.

Disk note (Oct 14): Karanbir's fp32 `model.onnx` and Surinder's ONNX export were deleted after the run for disk space. `karanbir-fp32` needs `hf download karansea/indicconformer-stt-pa-ctc-shabad-preview model.onnx` first. The two `.nemo` files are kept.

Venvs: `~/AAI/voice-venv` (onnxruntime, sentencepiece) and `~/AAI/nemo-venv` (nemo_toolkit[asr] 3.0.0, CPU torch).

Cost on this Mac (nice 19, 4 threads, busy machine): Karanbir int8/fp32 about 5 min each; a NeMo model about 33 min
per decoder; the akhand app replay about 10 min the first time and seconds after (stored).
