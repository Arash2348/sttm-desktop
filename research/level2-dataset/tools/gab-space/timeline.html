How this is scored, and credit
  • Each second takes the verdict of GAB's own scorer (ten 0.1 s samples, majority): right pangti = the words of the pangti being sung or read are on screen, or the screen moved to it less than 1 s ago (GAB's ON + CATCHING UP); otherwise right Shabad, other pangti / wrong Shabad / nothing shown (blank or the ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ slide) come from the Shabad answer key, so the strip agrees with the leaderboard's right-Shabad %.
  • Right Shabad (pangti not keyed): the recording has only a Shabad answer key here (most main-GAB kirtan), so pangti cannot be judged.
  • 1 s collar score (secondary, human pangti keys only): Karanbir Singh's benchmark metric, computed the way his eval.py does (it agrees with his scorer within 0.1 points on every GAB-Hard clip). Each whole second is a frame; inside a pangti only that pangti counts; within 1 s of a pangti change, or in a pause between pangtis, the pangti before or after or a blank screen also counts; seconds before the first and after the last pangti count as right. It is kinder than right pangti % (blank screens in pauses and edges count as right), so it reads higher.
  • Striped background = no answer key for that second (lead-in, silence, katha, the unlabelled tail): not scored.
  • Pangti answer keys: GAB-Hard = Jaspal's STTM Desktop sevadaar screen (human, may lag by a second or two). Pangti text comes from the STTM Desktop Gurbani database; a pangti is always shown whole.
  • Only practice clips (train + dev). The held-out test split is never shown. Replays come from cached model output (x86), the same runs as the leaderboard scorecards.
  • The per-second strip, the hover with the true vs shown pangti, and 1-second resolution follow Karanbir Singh's Live Captioning for Gurbani Kirtan benchmark v1 (code MIT, annotations CC BY 4.0) and its visualizations. No code or annotations are copied here; GAB keeps its own scoring (his uses a 1 s collar and accepts a blank in gaps between pangtis).
